Why "just practice more" isn't specific enough

Typing speed isn't one skill — it's dozens of small ones stacked together: how fast your fingers move between specific letter pairs, how often you fumble particular characters, whether your accuracy holds up as you speed up, whether your pace decays over a longer passage. Generic practice improves all of these a little. Targeted practice on your actual weak point improves the one thing that's genuinely holding your average down — which is a much faster path to a higher number.

The catch is that most people don't actually know which of those dozens of things is their real bottleneck. That's the part a deterministic diagnostic engine can answer that a "type more" tip never will.

How the diagnosis actually works

1
Take a normal typing test
No special setup — just type. Every keystroke's timing and correctness gets recorded locally as you go.
2
A deterministic engine finds your actual limiter
Not AI guesswork — real measured error rates per character, real measured delays per letter pair, compared against your own baseline. It checks accuracy, consistency, specific characters, punctuation, and endurance, in that priority order.
3
A tailored drill gets built from that exact finding
If your "wh" bigram is the bottleneck, the drill is packed with "wh" transitions — not generic word lists. You can also just ask the coach directly for a specific drill, and it'll build one grounded in your real data.
4
It keeps learning across sessions
A persistent weakness profile builds up over time, so the coach can work even if you haven't tested today — and you can see specific things you've actually gotten better at, like a bigram's timing dropping over the weeks.

Frequently asked

Is the diagnosis actually AI, or just a script?
The diagnosis itself — what your weak point is — is fully deterministic: real measured numbers, no AI guessing involved. The AI coach explains that finding in plain language and can hold a conversation about it, but it never invents the underlying data.
How many tests before it can diagnose anything useful?
A single test already produces a diagnosis for that session. A persistent, cross-session profile (so the coach works even without a fresh test) kicks in after about 5 completed tests.
Do I need to create an account?
No — you can test and get diagnosed immediately. An account just syncs your history and personal bests across devices.
